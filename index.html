What's inside

Understand a language, not just collect streaks

Follow the path from your first words to full sentences, or go straight to what you need.

Grammar

Grammar, explained

  • Full conjugation tables for the verbs you learn, tense by tense
  • Declension tables for languages with cases, like Polish, Ukrainian and German
  • Verb aspect, genders and articles, taught as real concepts rather than exceptions
  • Example sentences that show each pattern in use
Stories

Stories and conversations

Short stories and everyday conversations built from the words you're learning. Tap any word to see what it means and every form it takes, or listen to any line read aloud. Then practise each story three ways: fill in its missing words, rebuild its lines, then write parts of it from hearing.

Your level

Start where you are

A short level check places you on the path, so you don't start again from what you already know. Already know a lesson? Skip it. Not interested in one? Set it aside and bring it back any time.

The path

A clear path, and the freedom to leave it

The app always points to the lesson to do next. Or open Search and jump straight to one verb tense, the genitive case or the words for the kitchen: every lesson, word and story is there. Optional topic lessons have their own tab.

Memory

Practice that lasts

Every lesson comes back as you climb the path, a level harder each time, and spaced repetition puts the words due for review first. Producing an answer counts for more than recognising it, so your progress reflects what you can actually do.

Habit

A little every day

Home shows the lesson to go on with, your streak and the words you learned today. Guess the word from its meaning, letter by letter. Watch each module climb from Seen to Learned, Practised and Solid.

Practice

Eight ways to practise

Every session mixes them for you, starting gentle and moving to harder ways as a lesson's diamonds fill.

  1. Match the pairsForms and meanings on one board
  2. Pick the right answerClassic multiple choice
  3. Sort the pilesDeal each word onto its gender, number or aspect
  4. Fill the gapOne word is missing from a sentence
  5. Pick the endingPut the right ending on the stem
  6. Build the sentenceTap the words into the right order
  7. Spell the wordTap its letters in order
  8. Write what you hearListen, then write what you heard
