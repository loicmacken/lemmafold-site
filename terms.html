  • What it is. The free app gives you a daily amount of ink for practice; some lessons and games are Premium only. The app shows what is included before you buy. If Premium ends, you keep your progress and everything the free app offers.
  • Who sells it. Premium is sold and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), under their terms. We never see your payment details.
  • Price. The app and the store show the price, in your currency and including VAT, before you buy.
  • Free trial. New subscribers can start with a free trial, currently 7 days. The app shows the trial's length and the price after it before you start. If you do not cancel before the trial ends (on the App Store, at least 24 hours before), Premium starts and is charged.
  • Renewal. Premium is a monthly subscription. It renews automatically and the store charges you at the start of each month until you cancel. On the App Store, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current month to stop the next payment.
  • Cancelling. You can cancel at any time, online, in the store:
    • iPhone or iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LemmaFold
    • Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → LemmaFold
    • or in the app: You → Premium → Manage subscription
    The store confirms the cancellation. Premium stays until the end of the month you have paid for; after that, you have the free app.
  • Which devices. Premium belongs to the store account you bought it with, not to your LemmaFold account. It works on devices signed in to the same Apple Account or Google account (use Restore purchase on a new device). It does not carry over between the App Store and Google Play.
  • Codes. A code from us gives you Premium free for the period it names. After that it renews at the normal price unless you cancel.
  • Price changes. If the price goes up, the store tells you before you pay the new price, and you can cancel. Sometimes the store asks for your agreement first.
  • Withdrawal and refunds. In the EU you have the right to withdraw from a purchase within 14 days. Because the store sells Premium, you use that right, or ask for a refund, through the store: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google through your Google Play order history.
  • If we stop offering Premium, we tell you in the app at least 30 days before, and stop the renewals so you are not charged after it ends.
