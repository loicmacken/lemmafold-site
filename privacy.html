Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Who is responsible

LemmaFold is made by a sole trader in the Netherlands trading as LemmaFold (Chamber of Commerce (KvK) number 88506436). We decide how your data is used and are responsible for it (the "controller" under the GDPR). Questions and requests: privacy@lemmafold.com.

What we collect

  • If you never create an account: your lessons and progress stay on your phone, in a local database. Only crash reports and feedback you choose to send leave it (below).
  • If you create an account (email, Google or Apple sign-in): we store your email address, or the identifier your sign-in provider gives us, and your progress (lessons, practice, XP, streaks), so it syncs across your devices. With Apple you can hide your email; we then get an address that forwards to it. Supabase runs our accounts and sync, with the data in Frankfurt, Germany. Sign-in emails are sent by Mailjet (France).
  • Premium: Apple or Google sells Premium and handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. The app asks the store on your phone whether your subscription is active. The stores give us records of sales (such as the date, country, amount and an order number) for our accounts.
  • Crash reports: Sentry receives crash and error reports, stored in Frankfurt, to help us fix bugs. These can include device information and a stack trace of what the app was doing when it crashed.
  • App updates: when the app starts, it asks Expo whether a newer version of the app's code is available. This shares your IP address, your platform and the app's version.
  • Feedback you send: only when you use Send feedback in the app, or the flag on a question, lesson page, story or game. We receive what you write, the category you pick, for a report from the flag what it was about (the question, page, story or game - the course's own text, and for a question the answer you gave), and diagnostics: app version and build, platform, OS version and device model, the language you are learning, the screen you sent it from, whether you are signed in and when you last synced, your device's locale and time-zone offset, and a random id made by the app on your device. It never includes your email address, your account id or your progress. The report is stored as an issue in a private GitHub repository that only the developer can read. When a report leads to a change, its text and diagnostics may be given to an AI coding assistant (Anthropic's Claude) that helps write the fix. To limit abuse, a one-way keyed hash of your IP address (never the address itself) is kept.
  • Emails you send us, for example to support@lemmafold.com: what you write and your email address.
  • We do not run analytics, show ads, track you across other apps or websites, or sell any data. We collect no location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers.

Our legitimate interest is a working, safe app that improves from what learners tell us. You can object to it (see your rights).

You do not have to give us any data: the app works without an account. To have an account, we need an email address or your sign-in provider's identifier.

The app picks your next practice from your answers, on your phone. We make no decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects by automated means.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct it;
  • delete it (you can also delete your account yourself: You → Delete account in the app, or see how to delete your account);
  • limit how we use it;
  • give you your account data in a machine-readable format;
  • stop using it on the basis of our legitimate interest (object).

Email privacy@lemmafold.com. We answer within one month, free of charge, and may ask you to write from the email address of your account so we know the request is yours. Deleting the app removes all data kept on your phone.

If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or to the authority in the EU country where you live.

Children

LemmaFold is not meant for anyone under 16. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we tell you in the app before the change applies. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
