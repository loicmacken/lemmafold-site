Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

The short version

  • You can use all of LemmaFold without an account. Your progress then never leaves your device.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address and your progress so it can sync between your devices.
  • If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent so we can fix the bug.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

LemmaFold is the controller of the personal data described here. We are registered at the Chamber of Commerce in the Netherlands (KVK-nummer 88506436). You can reach us about anything in this policy at privacy@lemmafold.com.

What we collect

If you don't create an account

Lessons and your progress are stored only in a database on your device. We don't receive them. The only thing that can leave your device is a crash report (see below).

If you create an account

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address and account identifier they share with us (with Apple's Hide My Email, this is a relay address).
  • Learning progress: lesson progress and quiz scores, your review history for individual words, XP, streaks and achievements.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report with technical details: device model, operating system and app version, and where in the code the error happened. Reports do not include your email address or your progress, and are not linked to your account.

Emails you send us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers or browsing activity. The app contains no advertising or analytics software.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

Purpose Data Legal basis (GDPR)
Creating your account and signing you in Email address or sign-in identifier Performance of a contract (art. 6(1)(b))
Syncing your progress between devices Learning progress Performance of a contract (art. 6(1)(b))
Finding and fixing bugs Crash reports Legitimate interest in a working app (art. 6(1)(f))
Answering your questions Your emails Legitimate interest (art. 6(1)(f))

How long we keep it

Data Kept for
Account details and learning progress Until you delete your account, then removed immediately. We keep no backups of this data.
Crash reports 30 days, then deleted automatically
Server logs at our hosting provider At most 7 days
Support emails Up to 12 months after the conversation ends
Data on your device Until you sign out, delete your account or uninstall the app

Where a provider or its sub-processors handle data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

How we protect it

All data is encrypted in transit. Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your device, and the database only lets each account read and write its own rows.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including crash reporting;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To use any of these, email privacy@lemmafold.com. We answer within one month, free of charge.

You can delete your account yourself at any time - see how to delete your account.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; you can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live.

Children

LemmaFold is not directed at children under 16, and accounts should not be created by anyone under 16. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 16, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at privacy@lemmafold.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how your data is used in a significant way, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
