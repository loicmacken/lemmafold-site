Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version

  • You can use all of LemmaFold without an account. Your progress then never leaves your device.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address and your progress so it can sync between your devices.
  • If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent so we can fix the bug.
  • If you send feedback from the app, we receive what you write and some technical details, never your email address or progress.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Who we are

LemmaFold is the controller of the personal data described here. We are registered at the Chamber of Commerce in the Netherlands (KVK-nummer 88506436). You can reach us about anything in this policy at privacy@lemmafold.com.

What we collect

If you don't create an account

Lessons and your progress are stored only in a database on your device. We don't receive them. The only things that can leave your device are crash reports and feedback you choose to send (see below).

If you create an account

  • Account details: your email address. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the email address and account identifier they share with us (with Apple's Hide My Email, this is a relay address).
  • Learning progress: lesson progress and quiz scores, your review history for individual words, XP, streaks and achievements.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report with technical details: device model, operating system and app version, and where in the code the error happened. Reports do not include your email address or your progress, and are not linked to your account.

Feedback you send from the app

Only when you use Send feedback in the app, or the flag on a question, lesson page, story or game, we receive:

  • what you write and the category you pick;
  • for a report from the flag, what it was about: the question, page, story or game (the course's own text) and, for a question, the answer you gave;
  • diagnostics: app version and build, platform, operating system version and device model, the language you are learning, the screen you sent it from, whether you are signed in and when you last synced, your device's locale and time-zone offset, and a random id made by the app on your device.

Feedback never includes your email address, your account id or your progress. It is stored as an issue in a private GitHub repository that only the developer can read. When a report leads to a change, its text and diagnostics may be given to an AI coding assistant (Anthropic's Claude) that helps write the fix.

To limit abuse of the feedback form, we keep a one-way keyed hash of your IP address (never the address itself) for 24 hours.

Emails you send us

If you contact support, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

What we don't collect

No location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers or browsing activity. The app contains no advertising or analytics software.

How long we keep it

Data Kept for
Account details and learning progress Until you delete your account, then removed immediately. We keep no backups of this data.
Crash reports 30 days, then deleted automatically
Feedback from the app Until 90 days after the issue it opened is closed, then deleted
Keyed hash of your IP address 24 hours
Server logs at our hosting provider At most 7 days
Support emails Up to 12 months after the conversation ends
Data on your device Until you sign out, delete your account or uninstall the app

Who processes it

We use these service providers. Supabase and Sentry are bound by data processing agreements and keep your data in the EU. GitHub and Anthropic only ever receive feedback you choose to send, which carries no email address, account id or progress.

Provider What for Location Their policy
Supabase Account sign-in, progress sync and receiving feedback Frankfurt, Germany (EU) supabase.com/privacy
Sentry Crash reports Frankfurt, Germany (EU) sentry.io/privacy
GitHub Storing feedback you send United States GitHub privacy statement
Anthropic Helping write fixes from feedback United States anthropic.com/legal/privacy

If you choose Google or Apple sign-in, Google or Apple handle that sign-in under their own privacy policies. App store purchases and downloads are handled by Apple and Google as independent controllers.

Where Supabase's or Sentry's sub-processors handle data outside the European Economic Area, the transfer is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. Feedback you send is stored by GitHub, and may be read by Anthropic, in the United States. Please don't include personal details, such as your name or email address, in feedback.

How we protect it

All data is encrypted in transit. Your sign-in session is stored encrypted on your device, and the database only lets each account read and write its own rows.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have incorrect data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to how we use it, including crash reporting;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To use any of these, email privacy@lemmafold.com. We answer within one month, free of charge.

You can delete your account yourself at any time - see how to delete your account.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; you can also contact the authority in the EU country where you live.

Children

LemmaFold is not directed at children under 16, and accounts should not be created by anyone under 16. If we learn an account belongs to someone under 16, we will delete it. Parents can contact us at privacy@lemmafold.com.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how your data is used in a significant way, we will also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
