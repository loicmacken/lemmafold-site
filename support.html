Premium

How do I cancel my subscription?

Premium is billed by Apple or Google, so you cancel in your store's subscription settings. Uninstalling the app or deleting your account does not cancel it.

  • iPhone or iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions > LemmaFold (Apple's instructions)
  • Android: Google Play > your profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions (Google's instructions)

Premium stays active until the end of the period you have already paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Apple and Google handle refunds for purchases made through their stores: request a refund from Apple or see Google Play's refund policies. If something isn't working, please email us too, so we can fix it.

I have a new phone. How do I get Premium back?

Sign in to the same Apple or Google account you bought it with, then open Premium in the app and tap Restore purchase. A subscription bought on iPhone doesn't carry over to Android, or the other way round.

What is ink?

Without Premium, practice after the first module uses ink: 20 drops a day, one per practice question, refilled at midnight. A question you missed and see again later in the session is free. Reading lessons, words and stories never uses ink.

Your progress

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one, and your progress stays on your device. An account syncs your progress and streak between devices, so it also keeps them safe if you lose your phone.

Why is a line read aloud with no sound?

LemmaFold uses your device's own text-to-speech voices. If your device has no voice for the language, it stays silent; you can usually add one in your device's accessibility or language settings.
