Delete your LemmaFold account

App: LemmaFold · Developer: LemmaFold. You can delete your account at any time, with or without the app installed.

An account is optional in LemmaFold - it only exists to sync your progress between devices. If you never signed in, there is no account to delete: all your data lives on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open LemmaFold and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete account (below Sign Out) and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email (no app needed)

  1. Send an email to support@lemmafold.com with the subject "Delete my LemmaFold account".
  2. Send it from the email address linked to your account. If you can't (for example, you used Sign in with Apple with Hide My Email), say so and we will help you confirm the account is yours.

We delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply to confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, straight away:

  • Your account: your email address, or the identifier given by Google or Apple sign-in.
  • Your lesson progress and quiz scores.
  • Your word review history (which words you have practised and when).
  • Your XP, streaks and achievements.

We do not keep backups of the account database, so once deleted this data cannot be recovered - by you or by us.

When you delete from the app, your synced progress is also removed from that device. Other devices where you were signed in keep their local copy until you sign out or uninstall the app there.

What is kept, and for how long

Data Why Kept for
Crash reports (device model, OS and app version, technical error details) Fixing bugs. They contain no email address or progress and are not linked to your account. Deleted automatically after 30 days
Short-lived server logs at our hosting provider Security and keeping the service running Deleted automatically after at most 7 days
Your deletion request email (if you used option 2) A record that we handled your request Up to 12 months

Nothing else is kept. Lesson content itself is part of the app, not your account.
